## 1. Overview & Strategy
In accordance with the locked creative direction (*"Architecture Shaped by Place"*), visual quality is prioritized over aggressive lossy compression. High-fidelity WebP derivatives have been generated for the primary prioritized projects: **Möbius (On the path to Rediscovery)** and **Cultural Oasis**.

* **Color Management:** Direct sRGB rendering with preserved dynamic range and zero tint distortion.
* **Resampling Algorithm:** Lanczos filter (`Image.Resampling.LANCZOS`) ensuring fine architectural edges, timber grain, and text annotations remain razor-sharp.
* **Compression Profile:** WebP (`quality=88`, `method=6`, lossy with preserved fine details).
* **Source Archive Safety:** Read-only access to `client-assets/`. Zero client source files were modified, moved, renamed, converted in-place, or overwritten.
